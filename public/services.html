Our services

Everything you need for a spotless space

Five core services across Lagos mainland, Lekki, VI, Ibafo and Mowe. Every job is quoted personally — tell us about your space and we'll confirm scope and price.

H

Home & apartment cleaning

For occupied homes — flats, duplexes and estates. One-off deep cleans when you need a reset, or a regular plan so it never gets that far.

  • Bedrooms, living areas, kitchen and bathrooms
  • Dusting, vacuuming, mopping, surface sanitising
  • Inside appliances and behind furniture on deep cleans
  • Weekly, bi-weekly or monthly schedules
  • Move-in / move-out cleaning

Get a quote

P

Post-construction cleaning

After building or renovation work, the dust gets everywhere. We make a new or renovated space genuinely ready to move into.

  • Fine dust removal from all surfaces, fixtures and vents
  • Tile, grout and floor scrubbing; cement and paint residue
  • Window glass, frames and tracks
  • Sticker, label and adhesive removal
  • Final polish and walkthrough

Get a quote

U

Upholstery, sofa & carpet cleaning

Fabric-safe cleaning that lifts stains, dust and odours instead of pushing them deeper. Done in your home — no need to move furniture out.

  • Sofas, armchairs, dining chairs and headboards
  • Rugs, carpets and mattresses
  • Steam and extraction cleaning
  • Stain and odour treatment
  • Fabric checked before any product is used

Get a quote

S

Outsourced cleaning staff

Trained, supervised cleaners placed with offices, estates, schools and facilities — on a schedule you set, managed by us.

  • Daily, part-time or full-time placements
  • Identity-verified and trained staff
  • Supervision, quality checks and replacements handled by DELAKLEAN
  • Cleaning materials supply available
  • Monthly contract terms

Discuss a placement

F

Fumigation & pest control

Safe, approved treatments for homes and business premises, with clear guidance on re-entry times.

  • Cockroaches, ants, rodents, bedbugs and mosquitoes
  • Residential and commercial premises
  • Pre- and post-construction fumigation
  • Approved chemicals, safety briefing before treatment
  • Follow-up visit where needed
