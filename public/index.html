FAQ

Common questions

Which areas do you cover?

Lagos mainland, Lekki, Victoria Island, Ibafo and Mowe. If you're just outside these, ask — we can often arrange it.

How do I get a price?

Prices depend on the size and condition of the space, so we confirm every quote personally. Message us on WhatsApp with your location and what needs cleaning, and the team will respond with a clear quote.

Do I need to provide equipment or products?

No. Our cleaners bring professional equipment and cleaning products. If you have a preferred product for a specific surface, let us know.

Are your cleaners vetted?

Yes. Every cleaner is identity-verified, trained and supervised. Outsourced staff placements include supervision and replacement cover.

What are your working hours?

8am to 6pm, Monday to Saturday. Early-morning or after-hours slots for offices can be arranged on request.

Is fumigation safe for children and pets?

We use approved chemicals and advise on how long to stay out of treated areas. We'll confirm the specifics for your space before the visit.

Ready for a spotless space?

Message us now — we reply during business hours, usually within the hour.
