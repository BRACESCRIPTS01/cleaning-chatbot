About DELAKLEAN

We clean like it's our own space

A Lagos cleaning team built on showing up, doing the job properly, and not leaving until you've seen the result.

Our story

From one sofa to whole buildings

DELAKLEAN started with upholstery — sofas, rugs and mattresses that owners had given up on. Word travelled, and the same customers started asking us to handle their homes, their offices, and eventually the dust left behind after building and renovation work.

Today we cover five core services across Lagos mainland, Lekki, Victoria Island, Ibafo and Mowe, with the same rule we started with: no job is finished until the customer has walked through it with us.
